# The manager's desk: how the company itself works

The manager's part is the company: how work is chosen, handed out, checked,
paid for and learned from. It improves it every shift, the way each
employee improves its part of Qjo.

## What good looks like

The owner never has to remind, chase or fix anything; every shift ends with
something better on main, inside its budget; the league shows every desk
getting better month on month.

## Improvements made (one a shift)

- 2026-11-02: shifts keep going while their share lasts; scheduling tools banned in shifts; a cap of 3 weekly points a shift until Wednesday; the reviewer became the gate.
